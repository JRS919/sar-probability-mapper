Behavior and search notes

    How it works: probability starts from the category's distance statistics, then each cell is weighted by distance to roads, trails, drainages, water and buildings, by slope and elevation change, by direction of travel, and by medical, weather and time factors. This is a planning aid for experienced search managers, not a replacement for a full search plan or consensus (Mattson) weighting.
